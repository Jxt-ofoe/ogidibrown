Good to Know

Your Questions, Answered

Everything homeowners and business clients ask us before their first project in Kumasi and across Ghana.

What services does Ogidibrown 77 Enterprise offer?

We do all kinds of kitchen cabinets, CCTV installation, solar panels, wall panelling, wall cladding, ceiling design, cabinetry, wardrobe design, washroom finishing, and many more. One team handles your whole project from first sketch to final polish.

Where can your services be used in a home or office?

Our services work in any space: TV and feature walls, hallway and staircase cladding, sculpted ceilings, kitchen cabinets, wardrobe and cabinet fronts, full washroom finishes — as well as reception desks and facades in commercial spaces.

Are your finishes durable and easy to maintain?

Yes. Our wall panels and surfaces are hard-wearing and moisture-resistant, with non-porous finishes that simply wipe clean. Unlike paint they never need repainting, and unlike ceramic tiles there are no grout lines to scrub.

Do you work outside Kumasi?

Yes. Our office is at Sokoban Wood Village, Kumasi, but we take on residential and commercial projects across Ghana. Send us your location on WhatsApp and we will confirm coverage for your area.

How do I get a quote, and how long does installation take?

Message us on WhatsApp or call 055 121 4641 / 026 643 3578 with photos and rough measurements of your space. Quotes are free, and most feature walls and ceilings are completed within a few days once the design and materials are agreed.

What other services does Ogidibrown 77 Enterprise offer?

We do all kinds of kitchen cabinets, CCTV installation, solar panels, wall panelling, ceilings, wardrobes and furniture, bedroom and bathroom schemes, full commercial interiors, and many more — one team for the whole project.

Still deciding? Send us your space on WhatsApp — we reply within hours.
